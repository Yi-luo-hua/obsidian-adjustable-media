This update fixes two layout display issues in live preview.

- Move and resize handles outside layout frames are fully visible and clickable, including notes without floating layouts.
- Multiple blank lines above and below layouts retain their spacing. Consecutive floating layouts keep their existing arrangement when separated by a single blank line.

Original Markdown, layout dimensions and native whitespace behavior in reading view and PDF remain unchanged.

Resizing, cancellation and one-step undo were validated in Windows live preview. Mobile devices and other themes were not retested for this patch.
