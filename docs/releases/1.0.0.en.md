- **Position entire layouts freely.** In Live Preview, drag the top handle of a narrow, non-floating layout sideways to place it anywhere across the same line. Its relative position is saved in the note and carried over to Reading View and PDF exports.
- **Choose a layout border color.** Use **Layout border color** in the plugin settings to change the outline and glow, or switch back to Obsidian’s accent color.
- **More consistent text wrapping across views.** Reading View preserves consecutive blank lines after floating layouts. On the first switch to Reading View after editing, the editor stays visible until the new layout is ready, avoiding a blank flash and layout jump.
- **Fix the display of horizontal rules and the caret on empty lines.** Maple’s decorated horizontal rules are no longer clipped or covered by the next active line. The caret keeps its normal text height on empty lines, including with Cursor-Smith.
